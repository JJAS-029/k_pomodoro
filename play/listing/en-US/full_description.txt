Lab Pomodoro turns your focus sessions into lab experiments. While you work, the liquid in your flask evaporates; when you rest, it fills back up. And every minute you focus brings you closer to a new element of the periodic table.

🧪 A POMODORO TIMER THAT LOOKS AND FEELS DIFFERENT
• Tell it how many hours you want to work and get the whole plan: pomodoros, short and long breaks.
• Every session uses its own vessel and color: flasks, beakers and test tubes.
• Follow the time from the notification, a floating window, the widget or the Quick Settings tile.
• If Android closes the app or you restart your phone, your plan picks up where it left off.

⚛️ COLLECT THE PERIODIC TABLE
• Every 25 minutes of focus earns you a basic element, and every 60 minutes a rare one.
• Fuse elements in the synthesizer: their atomic numbers add up (H + U → Np). It's the only way to get the synthetic ones.
• Your elements fill your vessels and behave like the real thing: sodium fizzes, neon glows, mercury shines like metal.
• Tap any element to learn what it is, what it's used for, who discovered it and where.
• Level up each element's mastery to bronze, silver and gold.

🏅 MEDALS, STREAKS AND LEAGUES
• 17 medals: daily streaks, focus hours, early mornings, marathons, fusions and more.
• Optional weekly leagues: compete with up to 30 people on focus minutes and climb from Hydrogen to Platinum.
• Optional reminder so you don't break your streak.

📊 LEARN HOW YOU FOCUS
• Stats by day, month, weekday and hour: find out when you do your best work.
• Free cloud backup with your Google account, so you can switch phones without losing anything.

✨ LAB POMODORO PRO
• No ads.
• Ambient mode: just the vessel and the clock, dimmed, when you're not touching the screen.
• Focus sounds: white, pink and brown noise, rain and waves.
• Material You colors and realistic glass that bends the liquid like a lens.
• Places: find out where you focus best (your location stays on your phone).
• Export your history to CSV.

The app works offline and without an account. Available in English and Spanish.
